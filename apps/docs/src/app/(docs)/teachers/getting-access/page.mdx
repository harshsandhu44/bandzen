# Getting access

The CMS is where passages, questions, writing prompts, lessons and guides are
written and published. It is a separate site from the product, with its own
address, and it exists so content can change without a developer.

## The two roles

| Role        | Can do                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Teacher** | Create, edit, publish and delete every kind of content |
| **Admin**   | Everything a teacher can, plus grant and revoke roles  |

There is no third role and no per-section permission. A teacher has the same
reach over content as an admin — the only screen a teacher cannot open is the
one that hands out roles.

## Getting a role

Two steps, in this order:

1. **Sign up first.** A role is attached to an account that already exists, so
   an admin cannot grant you one before you have signed up.
2. **An admin grants it** by entering your email on the Teachers screen.

If your email is not found, it means the account does not exist yet — check
which address you signed up with.

## If you see "This account cannot edit content"

You are signed in, and the account you are signed in as has no CMS role.

<Callout title="The usual cause is the wrong account">
  The CMS and the product share one sign-in. If you use Bandzen as a candidate
  as well, you may be signed in on your candidate account. **Sign out and back
  in on the account that has the role** — the page offers sign out first for
  exactly this reason, and the footer shows which account you are on.
</Callout>

If it really is the right account, ask an admin to grant you the teacher role.

The page is a dead end by design: it does not redirect you anywhere, because
every other page in the CMS is behind the same check, and bouncing you between
them would be a loop rather than a fix.
