# Your study plan

The plan on Today is one task a day, chosen from what your marked work shows. It
is a set of rules, not a model — the same inputs always produce the same plan,
and nothing about it is generated text.

## How far ahead it looks

Up to **{PLAN_HORIZON_DAYS} days**, or the days until your test date, whichever
is shorter. Day one is today, not tomorrow.

If you have no test date, it runs a rolling fortnight and moves forward with
you.

## What it puts in a day

**Which skill.** If your reading and writing estimates differ by a whole band or
more, the weaker one gets two days in three. Otherwise the two alternate. A gap
of half a band is left alone — that is inside the noise of an estimate, and
chasing it would mean reacting to a single bad afternoon.

**Which drill.** Reading days rotate through drills aimed at your weakest
question type, timed passages, and full-length practice. Writing days rotate
through full-timing essays, intro-and-thesis-only work, and paragraph
development taken from a body paragraph a marker flagged. A drill is only
scheduled if content for it actually exists, so you will never be sent to a Task
1 drill on a day when there are no Task 1 prompts.

**Learn before drill.** If your weakest question type has a lesson you have not
read, the first reading slot teaches it instead of drilling it. Repeating a
technique you have not been shown is just repeating the mistake faster.

**The named weakness.** Your first writing task carries the actual thing the
marker found — not "Task 2 essay" but "Task 2 essay — focus: underdeveloped
body paragraphs".

## Why yesterday is not in it

The plan is rebuilt from today forward every time you open it. Nothing is
stored, and a past day's plan cannot be recovered.

That is deliberate, and it has a consequence worth knowing: **which module a
Tuesday in August asked for has no answer.** Whether you studied that Tuesday
does — that is what a [study day](/candidates/awards-and-streaks) records, and
it is the only thing about a past day that Bandzen keeps.

<Callout title="One task at a time">
  Exactly one task is ever active. The rest of the week is shown under "Coming
  up", greyed, so you can see the shape of it without being able to work ahead
  into a plan that will be rebuilt tomorrow anyway.
</Callout>
