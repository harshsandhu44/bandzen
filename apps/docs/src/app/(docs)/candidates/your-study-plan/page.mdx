# Your study plan

The plan on [Today](/candidates/today) is one task a day, chosen from what your
marked work shows. It is a set of rules, not a model — the same inputs always
produce the same plan, and nothing about it is generated text.

<Callout title="Not Speaking, for now">
  The plan responds to your Listening, Reading and Writing bands. Speaking is
  marked and shown on [Progress](/candidates/progress) like every other module,
  but it does not yet change what the plan asks you to do.
</Callout>

## How far ahead it looks

Up to **{PLAN_HORIZON_DAYS} days**, or the days until your test date, whichever
is shorter. Day one is today, not tomorrow.

If you have no test date, it runs a rolling fortnight and moves forward with
you.

## What it puts in a day

**Which skill.** The plan rotates through the skills you have a band for. If
your weakest is a whole band or more behind the next-weakest, it gets two days
in three and the third cycles through the rest. A gap of half a band is left
alone — that is inside the noise of an estimate, and chasing it would mean
reacting to a single bad afternoon. Before anything is measured, Reading and
Writing alternate.

**Which drill.** Reading days rotate through drills aimed at your weakest
question type, timed passages, and full-length practice. Writing days rotate
through four kinds: full-timing Task 2 essays, intro-and-thesis-only work,
paragraph development taken from a body paragraph a marker flagged, and a
full-timing Task 1 summary. A drill is only scheduled if content for it
actually exists, so you will never be sent to a Task 1 drill on a day when
there are no Task 1 prompts.

**Learn before drill.** If your weakest question type has a lesson you have not
read, the first reading slot teaches it instead of drilling it. Repeating a
technique you have not been shown is just repeating the mistake faster.

**The named weakness.** Your first writing task carries the actual thing the
marker found — not "Task 2 essay" but "Task 2 essay — focus: underdeveloped
body paragraphs".

## Why yesterday is not in it

The plan is rebuilt from today forward every time you open it. Nothing is
stored, and a past day's plan cannot be recovered.

That is deliberate, and it has a consequence worth knowing: **which module a
Tuesday in August asked for has no answer.** Whether you studied that Tuesday
does — that is what a [study day](/candidates/progress/awards-and-streaks)
records, and it is the only thing about a past day that Bandzen keeps.

<Callout title="One task at a time">
  Exactly one task is ever active. The rest of the week is shown under "Coming
  up", greyed, so you can see the shape of it without being able to work ahead
  into a plan that will be rebuilt tomorrow anyway.
</Callout>
