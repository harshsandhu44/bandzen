# The Today dashboard

Today is the app's front door — the first thing you see signed in, and the
first item in the sidebar. It is a summary, not a worksheet: everything on it
links out to the page that actually does the thing.

## Before your first diagnostic

Today shows a first-run state instead of the panels below: nothing has been
measured yet, so there is nothing to summarise. It points you at
[the diagnostic](/candidates/the-diagnostic).

## Once you have a band

**Continue** picks up an attempt you started and did not submit — the same one
that would be waiting for you if you opened the module directly.

**Today's task** is the one active item from
[your study plan](/candidates/your-study-plan), with a link straight into it.

**Band overview** shows your overall estimate against your target, and how far
your test date is if you gave one.

**Quick links** jump to each module — Reading, Listening, Writing, and Speaking
if you are Pro.

**Recent attempts** and **Coming up** are short lists: your last few marked
attempts, and the next couple of days your plan already knows about.

**Awards** shows the strip of badges you have earned, in the same order as the
full wall on [Progress](/candidates/progress/awards-and-streaks).

Nothing on Today is stored separately from the pages it summarises — change
your plan or mark a new attempt, and Today reflects it on the next load.
