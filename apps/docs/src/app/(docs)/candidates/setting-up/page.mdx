# Setting up

The first thing Bandzen asks for is six answers. They are the only things it
knows about you that it did not measure, and each one changes something you will
see later. You can change any of them afterwards in
[Settings](/candidates/settings).

## The six answers

**Exam type — Academic or General Training.** Decides which writing prompts you
are shown. Reading is currently marked on the Academic conversion scale
regardless; see [How reading is marked](/candidates/how-reading-is-marked).

**Target band.** Between 5.0 and 9.0, in half steps. It appears in the sidebar,
and Progress measures your estimates against it. It does not make the plan
harder or easier — the plan responds to what you score, not what you want.

**Your current level.** A rough guess is fine, and **I don't know** is a real
answer rather than a skipped question. Saying you don't know sends you straight
to [the diagnostic](/candidates/the-diagnostic), because the alternative is
planning around a number nobody has.

**Daily study minutes.** Sets the size of a day in your plan. A plan that asks
for forty minutes from someone with fifteen is a plan that gets abandoned.

**Test date.** Optional. With a date, your plan counts down to it and the
sidebar shows the days left. Without one, the plan runs a fortnight at a time,
rolling forward.

**Your timezone.** Captured from your browser rather than asked. It decides when
your day ends, which is what makes a
[study day](/candidates/awards-and-streaks) mean your Tuesday rather than a
server's.

## What happens when you finish

Two things.

You are granted <strong>{TRIAL_DAYS} days of Pro</strong>. It starts when you
finish setting up, not when your invitation was sent — the point is to see the
product working, and that clock should not run while you have not opened it.

Then you go to the diagnostic if you said you did not know your level, or to
Today if you gave one.
