# Bandzen Coach

Coach is a chat that knows how you are doing. It is a tutor, not an examiner.

## What it can see

Coach is given your situation from your own records before it answers anything:

- Your exam type, target band, test date and daily minutes.
- Your latest estimate for every module you have attempted — Reading,
  Listening, Writing, Speaking — or "never attempted" for one you have not.
- Your most recent report — the four criteria, their comments, and the
  weaknesses in it.
- Your reading accuracy, broken down by question type.

That context is assembled on the server from your rows. Nothing typed into the
chat can widen it, and Coach cannot see another candidate's work.

Coach's own replies render as formatted text — headings, lists, bold — rather
than a single block of prose. What you type stays plain.

## What it will not do

Coach is instructed not to claim to be an official examiner, not to guarantee
you a band, and not to invent a statistic. If it does not have a figure, it
says so rather than producing a plausible one.

It cannot mark an essay. Pasting one in gets you an opinion, not a report —
[submit it properly](/candidates/practice/writing) if you want it marked
against the criteria.

## Starting points

The six starters on the page are worth using, because they are the questions
Coach can answer best from what it has:

- What should I study today?
- Explain my latest test
- Why do I struggle with Matching Headings?
- How do I improve my Writing?
- Give me a Speaking Part 2 question
- What is the difference between Band 6 and Band 7 Writing?

A conversation carries **{COACH_MAX_TURNS} turns** of history. Past that, the
earliest exchanges drop out — so if a long conversation starts drifting, opening
a fresh one with a specific question works better than pushing on.

Your messages are not stored. Only the fact that a message was sent is
recorded, and only so the meter can count it.
