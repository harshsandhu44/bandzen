# Getting in

Bandzen is in a closed beta. You cannot sign up on your own — an account exists
because someone sent you an invitation.

## If you have an invitation

The invitation email carries a link. Follow it, create your account, and you
land on [setting up](/candidates/setting-up).

Your account is the email the invitation was sent to. If you sign in later with
a different email, that is a different account with none of your work in it.

## If you do not

The sign-up screen has a **No invitation?** form. Leave your email and it joins
the waitlist. That is the whole mechanism: the list records that you asked, and
invitations go out from it as places open up.

<Callout title="What the waitlist is not">
  Joining it does not create an account, hold a place, or start a trial. Nothing
  happens until an invitation arrives.
</Callout>

## Signing in later

Bandzen and the content CMS share one sign-in. That matters in one direction: if
you have also been given a teacher role, the same account reaches both. It does
not work the other way — being a candidate gives you nothing in the CMS, and
opening it signed in as a candidate shows a page telling you so. See
[Getting access](/teachers/getting-access) if that is what you were expecting.
