# Lessons

Lessons teach technique. They are grouped by module and then by depth, and they
are all free on every plan.

## The groups

**Foundations** — how the module works and how it is scored.
**Question types** — one lesson per type of question you will meet.
**Advanced** — locating evidence quickly, timing, and the things that separate a
6 from a 7.

## The six stages

Every lesson runs the same six stages, in order. The shape is the point: you are
never asked to try something you have not been shown, and you never finish
without checking whether it worked.

1. **Understand** — what the technique is and when it applies.
2. **See** — it done once, on a real example.
3. **Try** — you do one, with the answer and the reasoning available.
4. **Practice** — a button straight into the matching drill in Reading or
   Writing practice.
5. **Check** — what you should now be able to do.
6. **Improve** — where to go if it did not work.

The reader keeps a sticky rail of the six numbered stages alongside the text, so
you always know which one you are on and can jump back to an earlier one.

## Finishing one

**Mark as read** at the foot records it. Two things follow from that: the lesson
index shows how many of a module's lessons you have finished, and the day counts
as a [study day](/candidates/progress/awards-and-streaks) — reading a lesson
keeps a streak alive exactly as sitting an attempt does.

There is no test at the end and nothing checks that you read it. Marking it read
is a claim you make about yourself.

## Not written yet

<Callout tone="locked" title="Not written yet">
  Some lessons are listed with their title and no link. That means the lesson is
  planned and has not been written. It is shown rather than hidden so you can
  see the shape of what is coming, and it is not a link because there is nothing
  behind it.
</Callout>

Listening and Speaking have module cards on the Learn hub like Reading and
Writing do, but their lesson catalogues are the newest — a card that says
"Lessons coming soon" rather than listing lessons is that module, not a locked
state.
