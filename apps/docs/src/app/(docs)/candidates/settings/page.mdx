# Settings

Three tabs — Preparation, Plan & billing, Account.

## Preparation

The same fields from [setting up](/candidates/setting-up), shown all at once
rather than as a wizard, since there is nothing to build toward here. Anything
can change at any time.

What each change does:

| Change        | Effect                                                                           |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exam type     | Which writing prompts you are shown                                              |
| Target band   | The sidebar figure and the comparison on Progress                                |
| Current level | Nothing, once you have been measured — real attempts replace it                  |
| Daily minutes | The size of a day in your plan                                                   |
| Test date     | Adding one makes the plan count down; removing it returns to a rolling fortnight |
| Timezone      | When your day ends, and so which day a study day falls on                        |

<Callout title="Changing your timezone">
  Study days are calendar days in your timezone. Moving it shifts where the
  boundaries fall, which can change how a past run of days is counted. Awards
  you have already earned are never taken back — see [Awards and
  streaks](/candidates/progress/awards-and-streaks).
</Callout>

None of this deletes anything. Your attempts, reports and awards are untouched
by editing your profile.

## Plan & billing

Whether you are on Pro and the date it runs to, and the cancel dialog. The
dialog states what you keep and what you lose before you confirm it — see
[Plans and limits](/candidates/plans-and-limits).

## Account

Your name, email and password, managed through the sign-in provider rather
than this form — Settings links out to them rather than editing them directly.
The light and dark theme toggle, a link to this documentation, the app
version, and sign out.

The theme toggle and sign out are also reachable from the account menu in the
persistent top bar — the only route to them on a phone, where Settings is
behind the gear icon rather than a tab of its own.

The version is worth knowing about: if you are reporting something that looks
wrong, the version string says which build you saw it on.
