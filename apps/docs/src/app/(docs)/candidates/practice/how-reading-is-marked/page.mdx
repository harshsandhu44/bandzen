# How reading is marked

Reading marking is a pure function. No model is involved, the answer key is
fixed, and the same answers would produce the same score every time.

The same function marks [Listening](/candidates/practice/listening), on the
same conversion table below — everything on this page applies there too.

## How your answer is compared

Every question's key holds **every accepted form** of the answer, so both
"cotton" and "raw cotton" can be right where both are.

Comparison ignores capitalisation and surrounding whitespace. Typing ` TRUE`
is not a mistake and is not marked as one.

## The review

The review page shows:

- Your **band**, and the raw score behind it, labelled as an estimate rather
  than a score.
- Every question, marked correct or incorrect.
- For each one, the **evidence sentence** — the line in the passage that makes
  the answer the answer — and an explanation of why.
- **Recurring patterns**: question types you have attempted at least 5
  questions of and are getting fewer than 75% right, across every attempt you
  have made, not just this one.

The evidence sentence is the part worth slowing down on. Getting a question
wrong tells you little; seeing the sentence you needed to have found, and
realising you read past it, tells you what to do differently next time.
