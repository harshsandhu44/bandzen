# Reading practice

Reading practice is unlimited on every plan, including Free. Marking a reading
attempt costs nothing to run, so capping it would only throttle the habit worth
building.

## Finding a passage

The list can be filtered by **question type** and by **difficulty**. Arriving
from a plan task narrows it to the one passage that task means, and arriving
from a lesson's Practice stage preselects that lesson's question type.

## Sitting it

A passage carries 13 questions and a 20-minute timer. Timing is proportional to
a full test — 40 questions in 60 minutes — so a shorter set gets a
correspondingly shorter clock rather than an arbitrary one.

While you work:

- **Flag** a question to come back to it. Flags are yours and are not marked.
- **Answers save as you type**, debounced. Saving is never blocked by anything,
  including a spent quota.
- **Leaving is safe.** An attempt you have started but not submitted is still
  there when you come back, with your answers and your flags in it. Starting
  from the list resumes it rather than beginning a second one.

## Submitting

Submitting asks you to confirm, and the confirmation names how many questions
are unanswered. Once submitted, an attempt is marked immediately and cannot be
reopened.

Then you land on the review — see
[How reading is marked](/candidates/how-reading-is-marked) for what is on it.

<Callout title="Reading practice never expires">
  There is no weekly limit, no Pro gate and no quota meter on reading. The two
  things that are metered on Free are essay marking and Coach, because those
  cost money to run. See [Plans and limits](/candidates/plans-and-limits).
</Callout>
