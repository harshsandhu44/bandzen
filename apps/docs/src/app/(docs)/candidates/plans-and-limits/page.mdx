# Plans and limits

## What Free includes

Two things are metered, and only two: **marked essays** and **Coach messages**.
They are the two things that cost real money every time they run. Speaking
sits apart from both — it is not metered, it is closed entirely on Free.

|                          | Free                                     |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Marked essays            | {FREE.essays} per {FREE.windowDays} days |
| Coach messages           | {FREE.coach} per {FREE.windowDays} days  |
| Diagnostic               | {FREE.diagnostics}, ever                 |
| Band history on Progress | Last {FREE.trendPoints} attempts         |
| Reading practice         | Unlimited                                |
| Listening practice       | Unlimited                                |
| Lessons                  | Unlimited                                |
| Guides                   | Unlimited                                |
| Speaking                 | Not available                            |

Reading, Listening, lessons and guides are unlimited on purpose. Marking them
costs nothing to run, and capping the thing that builds the habit would be
throttling the reason to be here at all.

## The window is rolling

The {FREE.windowDays}-day window rolls; it is not a calendar week resetting on
Mondays. Your meter always shows the exact day yours resets — "Thursday" while
that is unambiguous, a date once it is not.

## Your trial

Finishing [setting up](/candidates/setting-up) grants **{TRIAL_DAYS} days of
Pro**. It starts then rather than when you signed up, so the clock does not run
while you have not opened the product.

## What Pro changes

Unlimited essay marking, unlimited Coach, your full band history on Progress,
retaking the diagnostic, and **Speaking** — the whole module, not a bigger
meter on it.

Prices change, so they are not written here. They are on the
[pricing section of the Bandzen site]({PRICING_URL}), which is where they are
kept up to date. The upgrade page personalises its pitch with the days left to
your test date if you gave one.

## Cancelling

<Callout title="You keep the period you paid for">
  Cancelling does not end your access. It stops the renewal. Pro runs to the end
  of the period already paid for, and then you are on Free.
</Callout>

After that: {FREE.essays} essay marks and {FREE.coach} Coach messages a week,
your band history goes back to the last {FREE.trendPoints} attempts, and
Speaking closes — starting a new attempt is blocked the same way it is for any
Free account. Reading practice, Listening practice, and lessons stay
unlimited.

**A failed payment is not an instant cutoff.** Access is decided by the date your
period runs to, and a failed renewal simply does not extend it — so the retry
window is a grace period at no cost to you.

Refunds are available within **{REFUND_DAYS} days**.
