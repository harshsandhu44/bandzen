# The diagnostic

The diagnostic is the first measurement. Until you have taken it, the plan on
Today is a sensible default rather than yours, and Progress has nothing to draw.

## What it consists of

A trimmed version of the full mock — all four skills, chained, in one sitting,
free on every plan:

| Part | What                          | Time       |
| ---- | ----------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1    | Two listening sections        | ~20 min    |
| 2    | Two reading passages          | ~40 min    |
| 3    | A Task 2 essay                | 40 min     |
| 4    | A Parts 1–3 speaking interview | ~10 min    |

You are asked for a target band and a test date first, if you have not already
given them.

Each section is recorded as its own attempt linked to the sitting, which is why
the result page shows them as one sitting and one overall band while Progress
still counts each toward its own module.

## Your result

Listening and Reading are marked the moment you submit them. Writing and
Speaking go off to a language model, which takes up to a minute each, so the
result page fills in those bands when they land. You can close the tab; the
marking finishes without you.

The overall figure is the mean of the four. From it, Bandzen builds
[your study plan](/candidates/your-study-plan) — and the plan is where the
diagnostic actually pays off, because it knows which skills are behind and by
how much.

## Taking it more than once

<Callout tone="pro" title="Pro">
  Free includes {FREE.diagnostics} diagnostic. Retaking is a Pro feature. If you
  have already been measured, the page offers **Retake with Pro** and a link
  back to your last result, rather than letting you start one and stopping you
  at the end.
</Callout>

Retaking is not usually the useful move anyway. A second diagnostic measures the
same skills the same way; ordinary practice attempts feed Progress and the plan
just as well, within the {FREE.practicePerModule} Reading and Listening tests
Free includes.

## Where it sits in Practice

["Sit a test"](/candidates/practice) on the Practice hub lists the diagnostic
alongside its section, duration, difficulty and whether you have taken it —
next to the full four-skill mock test.
