# Progress

Progress is everything Bandzen has measured about you. Every figure on it comes
from an attempt you completed. Nothing on it is projected.

## Estimated band over time

Your marked attempts, oldest first, against the 0–9 scale.

<Callout tone="pro" title={`Free: your last ${FREE.trendPoints} attempts`}>
  Free shows the most recent {FREE.trendPoints}, and says how many earlier ones
  are not shown rather than quietly cropping the line. Pro shows all of them.
</Callout>

## Per-module bands

Reading and writing, each on its own scale, against your target. These are the
two numbers [your study plan](/candidates/your-study-plan) compares to decide
which skill gets more of your week.

## The reading skill matrix

Your accuracy on each question type, across every attempt.

A type only appears with a figure once you have attempted at least
**{MIN_ATTEMPTED} questions** of it. Below that it says so instead. Two wrong
answers out of three is not a weakness, it is a Tuesday — and a recommendation
built on it would be a guess wearing a percentage sign.

## Patterns, and the award wall

**Patterns across your attempts** names the thing that keeps recurring rather
than the thing that went wrong most recently.

**The award wall** shows all eight awards, earned and unearned, with the rule
for each one visible whether or not you have it. See
[Awards and streaks](/candidates/awards-and-streaks).

## Activity

Tests completed, questions attempted, minutes in tests, and lessons finished.

**Minutes count time inside timed attempts only.** Nothing measures how long you
spend reading a lesson, so lesson time is left out rather than estimated. The
figure is smaller than the time you have actually put in, and it is honest about
what it counts.

## What Progress will not show you

There is no projected band and no "you are 0.5 from your target, here is when
you will get there" line.

Bandzen has no basis on which to promise a gain. It can tell you what you scored,
what you keep getting wrong, and what to do next. A prediction about exam day
would be a number with nothing behind it, and this product does not show numbers
it cannot defend.
