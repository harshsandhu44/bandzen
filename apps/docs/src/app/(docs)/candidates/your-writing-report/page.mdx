# Your writing report

The report is what a marker made of your essay. It arrives in up to a minute,
and you do not have to wait on the page for it.

## The three states

**Marking.** "Reading your response" with an indeterminate bar. You can close the
tab — the marking finishes without you and the report is there when you come
back.

**Failed.** Marking errored. Your response is saved and nothing is lost, and
there is a **Mark it again** button.

<Callout title="A failed run has never counted against your weekly marks">
  Retrying costs you nothing. The mark was charged when you started the attempt,
  and a run that failed did not consume a second one.
</Callout>

**Done.** The report itself.

## What is in it

**An overall band**, shown against the 0–9 scale. It is the mean of the four
criteria, rounded to the nearest half band — not a separate judgement layered on
top of them.

**The four criteria**, each with its own band and a comment saying what earned
it:

<ul className="mt-4 space-y-2 pl-5 text-[0.9375rem] leading-7 list-[square] marker:text-chrome">
  {WRITING_CRITERIA.map((criterion) => (
    <li key={criterion}>{criterion}</li>
  ))}
</ul>

**Annotations** — between four and eight short extracts, quoted **verbatim from
your own essay**, each marked as one of:

| Mark          | Means                         |
| ------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Works well    | Do more of this               |
| Error         | A concrete mistake, named     |
| Needs support | A claim that goes undeveloped |

Every quote is checked against your essay before you see it, and any the marker
produced that is not literally in your text is dropped. A missing annotation is
better than an invented one.

**Strengths and weaknesses**, as short lists.

## What the report is not

It is an estimate produced by a language model reading against the published
band descriptors. It is not an IELTS score, and no examiner has seen your essay.
Two markings of the same essay can differ slightly, which is worth remembering
before reading much into a half-band move between two essays.

The thing to act on is not the number. It is the lowest criterion and the
comment attached to it — that comment is what
[your study plan](/candidates/your-study-plan) reads to name your next writing
task.
