# Awards and streaks

Awards mark study habit, not performance. None of them depends on your band, and
there are no points and no XP — this product does not show numbers it cannot
defend from a record.

## What a study day is

> A study day is a calendar day, in your timezone, on which you completed an
> attempt or finished a lesson.

That is the whole unit, and three things follow from it:

- **Your timezone, not a server's.** This is what
  [setting up](/candidates/setting-up) captures it for.
- **An attempt and a lesson on the same afternoon are one study day.** The unit
  is the day, not the amount.
- **An essay counts when marking finishes**, not when you submit it. Submitting
  at 11:50pm and marking landing at 12:01am makes it the next day's study day.

## Streaks are kept once earned

The streak awards read your **longest streak ever**, not your current one.

Missing a day ends a run. It never takes back an award you have already got.

<Callout title="Why it works that way">
  People sit this exam on a date they cannot move. A system that confiscates
  progress for a missed day punishes exactly the weeks — illness, work, a family
  emergency — when someone most needs a reason to come back. So a run that ended
  is still a run that happened.
</Callout>

The **current** streak in the header is display only. No award depends on it,
and a run that ended yesterday still shows, because at nine in the morning
today's studying has not happened yet.

## Where you see them

**On Today**, as a strip when you earn something new. It is a quiet status
message rather than a popup — an award is worth mentioning, not worth
interrupting someone for. **Got it** dismisses everything unseen at once.

**On Progress**, as the award wall: all eight, earned ones dated, unearned ones
dimmed but with their rule showing. A wall of mysteries motivates nobody.
