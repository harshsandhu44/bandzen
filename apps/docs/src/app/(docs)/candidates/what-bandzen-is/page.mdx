# What Bandzen is

Bandzen is an IELTS preparation product. You practise on real question types,
your work is marked, and what the marking finds decides what you are asked to
do next.

## What is built

All four modules — **Reading**, **Listening**, **Writing** and **Speaking** —
have a marking engine behind them.

Reading and Listening are marked by a pure function — no model is involved,
the answer key is fixed, and the same attempt would score the same every time.
Writing and Speaking are marked by a language model against the IELTS band
descriptors: Writing reads your essay, Speaking listens to your recording
directly. Both reports name four criteria, quote your own answer back to you,
and say what to fix.

<Callout tone="pro" title="Speaking is Pro">
  Reading, Listening and Writing are on every plan, including Free. Speaking is
  the one module Free cannot open — see [Plans and
  limits](/candidates/plans-and-limits).
</Callout>

Everything else follows: your estimated band, your study plan, the skill
matrix on Progress, and what Coach knows about you.

## What is not built

<Callout tone="locked" title="Not built">
  There is no single sitting that chains all four modules into one mock test.
  [The diagnostic](/candidates/the-diagnostic) — one reading passage and one
  Task 2 essay, sat under timing — is the closest thing today.
</Callout>

Reading practice is currently marked on the Academic conversion scale for both
Academic and General Training candidates, and so is Listening. General
Training converts differently and more generously; until that ships, read a GT
reading or listening band as conservative.

Your study plan currently responds to your Reading and Writing bands only.
Listening and Speaking are marked and shown on
[Progress](/candidates/progress), but they do not yet change what the plan
asks you to do next.

## Estimates, not scores

Every band Bandzen shows you is an estimate produced by this product. It is not
an IELTS score, it does not come from IELTS, and no one here can tell you what
you will get on the day.

> Bandzen is an independent IELTS preparation platform and is not affiliated
> with or endorsed by IELTS, the British Council, IDP, or Cambridge University
> Press & Assessment. Band estimates provided by Bandzen are for practice
> purposes and are not official IELTS scores.

The product holds itself to that everywhere. Progress shows no projected band
and no "you could reach 7.0" line, because there is no basis on which to promise
a gain. Activity counts minutes spent inside timed attempts only, because nobody
measures how long you spend reading a lesson, and an estimate there would be a
guess dressed as a figure.
