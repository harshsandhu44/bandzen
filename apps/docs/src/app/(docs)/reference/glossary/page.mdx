# Glossary

**Attempt** — one sitting of one thing: a reading passage or an essay. It has a
start, a submission and a mark. An attempt you have started but not submitted is
still an attempt, and it resumes where you left it.

**Estimated band** — a 0–9 figure this product produced from your work. Not an
IELTS score. See [The band scale](/reference/band-scale).

**Diagnostic** — the first measurement: one reading passage and one Task 2
essay, in one sitting. See [The diagnostic](/candidates/the-diagnostic).

**Drill** — a practice task aimed at one thing, as opposed to a full-length
timed attempt. "Matching headings drill" is a drill; "full passage timed" is
not.

**Technique** — what a [lesson](/candidates/lessons) teaches. The plan teaches a
technique before drilling the question type it belongs to.

**Marked** — read and scored. Reading is marked by a fixed function; writing is
marked by a language model against the band descriptors. "Marking" is the act;
a "mark" on Free is one of your weekly essay allowance.

**Report** — what marking an essay produces: an overall band, four criteria,
annotations on your own sentences, strengths and weaknesses. See
[Your writing report](/candidates/your-writing-report).

**Review** — what marking a reading attempt produces: your answers against the
key, with the evidence sentence and an explanation for each.

**Study day** — a calendar day, in your timezone, on which you completed an
attempt or finished a lesson. The unit every streak and award is counted in. See
[Awards and streaks](/candidates/awards-and-streaks).

**Streak** — consecutive study days. Awards read your longest streak ever, so
missing a day ends a run but never takes back an award.

**The plan** — the one-task-a-day schedule on Today, rebuilt from today forward
every time it is read. See [Your study plan](/candidates/your-study-plan).

**Pro** — the paid plan. Removes the two meters on Free and opens your full band
history. See [Plans and limits](/candidates/plans-and-limits).

**Quota** — what is left of a metered allowance in the current rolling window,
shown with the exact day it resets.

**Draft / published** — whether content is visible to candidates. Candidates see
published content only. See [Draft and published](/teachers/draft-and-published).

**Teacher** — someone with a CMS role, who writes and publishes content.
Teachers have no roster and no view of individual candidates; Bandzen has no
concept of a class. See [Getting access](/teachers/getting-access).
