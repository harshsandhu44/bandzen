# Glossary

**Attempt** — one sitting of one thing: a reading passage, a listening track,
an essay, or a speaking test. It has a start, a submission and a mark. An
attempt you have started but not submitted is still an attempt, and it resumes
where you left it.

**Estimated band** — a 0–9 figure this product produced from your work. Not an
IELTS score. See [The band scale](/reference/band-scale).

**Diagnostic** — the first measurement: one reading passage and one Task 2
essay, in one sitting. See [The diagnostic](/candidates/the-diagnostic).

**Drill** — a practice task aimed at one thing, as opposed to a full-length
timed attempt. "Matching headings drill" is a drill; "full passage timed" is
not.

**Technique** — what a [lesson](/candidates/learn/lessons) teaches. The plan
teaches a technique before drilling the question type it belongs to — true for
Reading, which is the one module the plan drills this way today.

**Marked** — read and scored. Reading and Listening are marked by a fixed
function; Writing and Speaking are marked by a language model against the band
descriptors — Writing reads your essay, Speaking listens to your recording
directly. "Marking" is the act; a "mark" on Free is one of your weekly essay
allowance.

**Report** — what marking an essay or a speaking test produces: an overall
band, four criteria, annotations on your own words, strengths and weaknesses.
See [Your writing report](/candidates/practice/your-writing-report).

**Review** — what marking a reading or listening attempt produces: your
answers against the key, with the evidence sentence and an explanation for
each.

**Study day** — a calendar day, in your timezone, on which you completed an
attempt or finished a lesson. The unit every streak and award is counted in.
See [Awards and streaks](/candidates/progress/awards-and-streaks).

**Streak** — consecutive study days. Awards read your longest streak ever, so
missing a day ends a run but never takes back an award.

**The plan** — the one-task-a-day schedule on Today, rebuilt from today forward
every time it is read. Reads Reading and Writing bands only. See
[Your study plan](/candidates/your-study-plan).

**Pro** — the paid plan. Removes the two meters on Free, opens your full band
history, and unlocks Speaking. See [Plans and limits](/candidates/plans-and-limits).

**Quota** — what is left of a metered allowance in the current rolling window,
shown with the exact day it resets.

**Draft / published** — whether content is visible to candidates. Candidates see
published content only. See [Draft and published](/teachers/draft-and-published).

**Teacher** — someone with a CMS role, who writes and publishes content.
Teachers have no roster and no view of individual candidates; Bandzen has no
concept of a class. See [Getting access](/teachers/getting-access).
